## Research design caveats

These ten are *candidates*, not ten confirmed PCC-positive gold-standard studies. Before using them to calculate search sensitivity, independently inspect abstracts/full texts and classify as positive or boundary/negative. Seed selection must not be based on the candidate search's results. The pool should ideally add one or more clearly eligible completed practice-based doctoral theses if these can be identified and verified. Note that article DOI embedded years may differ from online publication year; use publisher publication metadata for eligibility.

Use Aref & Stensaker (2025), https://doi.org/10.1080/03075079.2025.2605101, as an existing review/citation-mining resource rather than a presumed included primary study.

Next: confirm at least 6–8 unambiguously PCC-positive records, establish their indexing in Scopus/ERIC, and test a sensitive two-block baseline and optional institutional third block; document retrieval and missed records.
