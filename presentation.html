From Policy Commitment to Institutional Enactment

Learner-centredness in Irish higher education

Shaun Ferns · Technological University Dublin

What is the PhD about?

How does learner-centredness become institutional practice?

Irish higher education institutions make commitments to learner-centredness. I am investigating how those commitments are interpreted and translated through policy, governance, curriculum and everyday institutional decisions.

What happens between a commitment on paper and the experience of learners?
