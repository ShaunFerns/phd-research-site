# PhD Research Site

This repository hosts a public-facing overview of Shaun Ferns' PhD research on learner-centredness as institutional enactment in Irish higher education.

## Live site

[Open the PhD research site](https://shaunferns.github.io/phd-research-site/)

[Open the reveal.js presentation](https://shaunferns.github.io/phd-research-site/presentation.html)

## Current public status — November 2026

The project is in its first-year evidence-building phase. Current work includes:

- a JBI-guided scoping review reported using PRISMA-ScR;
- a longitudinal public-document corpus across Irish higher education institutions, sectors and organisational transitions;
- development of the analytical and methodological basis for later comparative case-study research;
- continued testing of the proposed practice-based documentary strand rather than fixing its final form in advance.

The public site records selected research progress only. Detailed protocols, search histories, screening records, methodological memos, supervision materials and research data are maintained separately in a private version-controlled workspace.

## Site structure

- `index.html` — landing page and research overview
- `presentation.html` — reveal.js progress presentation
- `assets/site.css` — shared styling

## Public-facing boundary

This repository should not contain formal ethics documentation, participant data, privileged institutional records, supervision notes or licensed research datasets.
