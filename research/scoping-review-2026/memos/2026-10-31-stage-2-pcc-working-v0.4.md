# Stage 2 — PCC and eligibility boundary (working v0.4)

**Date:** 31 October 2026  
**Status:** Provisional design for supervisory review; not final eligibility rules.

## PCC

- **Population:** Higher education institutions, institutional communities and relevant actors involved in learner-centred educational arrangements. No particular participant group is required.
- **Concept:** Institutional interpretation, translation, negotiation or enactment of learner-centredness. The study must substantively investigate an institutional relationship; a named formal policy is not required.
- **Context:** Recognised higher education institutions internationally, including institutional, faculty/school, programme, curriculum and educational-practice settings where their institutional relationship is investigated.

## Working operational definition

Institutional enactment of learner-centredness refers to processes through which learner-centred educational commitments, principles or orientations are interpreted, negotiated, translated, organised or realised within higher education institutions, including how organisational conditions, actors and institutional arrangements influence those processes.

## Screening principle to test

A source should qualify on the Concept only when it provides (a) a substantive learner-centred educational connection and (b) an analysis of an institutional or organisational relationship. A routine classroom intervention does not qualify simply because it occurs in a university. An institution-level student participation study likewise does not automatically qualify unless a learner-centred educational relationship can be established. These are working safeguards to be tested on borderline cases.

The review should not require: a formal policy document; an institutional leader as a participant; explicit invocation of policy enactment theory; evidence on all analytical dimensions; or a particular level of institutional hierarchy.

## Open questions

1. How explicit must the learner-centred connection be? Avoid author-term-only restrictions while preventing retrospective researcher attribution.
2. Do conceptual papers qualify only when they advance an institutionally situated analytical argument supported by a discernible scholarly basis?
3. Should research reviews count as included evidence or serve citation-search and contextual functions? Address at the publication-type stage, avoiding double counting.
4. Define and justify the boundary of recognised higher education provision, including international differences.
5. Publication types, language limits and dates remain to be justified separately; information sources and search strings follow the PCC decision.

## Reflective note

A broad population and international context do not necessarily create an unmanageable review if the Concept has a clear and consistently applied institutional relationship requirement. However, institutional relevance alone is insufficient: studies must also investigate learner-centred educational commitments, principles or orientations rather than treating any participation or pedagogical innovation as synonymous with learner-centredness. Calibration should deliberately test both false inclusions and false exclusions.

## Addendum — eligible research approaches (31 October 2026)

Practice-based and practice-led research are explicitly eligible alongside qualitative, quantitative, mixed-methods, action, participatory, design-based and relevant conceptual/theoretical research. Eligibility is determined by the review's substantive learner-centred and institutional relationship criteria, not by a hierarchy of research designs. Practice-based work should make an identifiable research contribution rather than consist only of an unanalysed description of an artefact or activity. Research approach will be recorded during data charting, permitting examination of how enactment has been investigated across methodological traditions. This inclusion decision does not confer preferential treatment on practice-based studies.
